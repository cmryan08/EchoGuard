About prolonged sitting

The World Health Organization's 2020 guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour recommend that adults limit the amount of time they spend being sedentary, and note that replacing sedentary time with physical activity of any intensity, including light intensity, provides health benefits.

In a laboratory study, Dunstan and colleagues (2012) compared uninterrupted sitting with sitting broken up by short bouts of light or moderate walking in overweight and obese adults. Breaking up sitting reduced the rise in blood glucose and insulin after meals.

This app counts standing and moving together as "upright". Standing still is not the same as physical activity; the studies above concern reducing and interrupting sitting time.

Talk to your healthcare provider for advice about your situation.

References

  1. World Health Organization. WHO guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour. Geneva: World Health Organization; 2020.
  2. Dunstan DW, Kingwell BA, Larsen R, et al. Breaking up prolonged sitting reduces postprandial glucose and insulin responses. Diabetes Care. 2012;35(5):976-983.

What is collected

  • Only time spent sitting, standing, moving and away, and how you responded to stand reminders.
  • Stored locally on this computer in data/posture.sqlite3. Simulated and replayed data are never saved.
  • No camera, no audio. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically; you decide what to download and share.

Method

Sitting, standing, moving and away are estimated from changes in Wi-Fi channel state information between two ESP32-S3 boards, using a per-room calibration recorded by the user. No camera or microphone is used. Estimates depend on the room and board placement and may be wrong.

Disclaimer: Research prototype. Not a medical device. Predictions are not medically validated. This summary is not a diagnosis.

Calibrate the room

Calibration teaches the app what your room looks like empty, with you walking, standing still and sitting. It runs by itself after you press Start. You will hear a beep and a spoken instruction at every step.
